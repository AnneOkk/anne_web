## Supervision


**PhD co-supervision** on human–AI interaction in healthcare: AI decision support for mental healthcare (LMU Munich, 2022–2026) and AI decision support in ophthalmology (TH Augsburg, since 2022).

**Master's theses** on, for example, technostress among leaders through AI integration, meta-analyses of future work self salience, virtual patient simulations in psychotherapy training, and predictors of clinicians' intention to use AI-enabled decision support. Several theses were graded 1.0 and contributed to publications in the *Journal of Medical Internet Research* and the *Journal of Vocational Behavior* and to a meta-analysis currently under review.

**Bachelor's theses** on entrepreneurs' error appraisal and coping, work events and job satisfaction, and ambidexterity and entrepreneurial performance (University of Groningen).

I welcome thesis students interested in human–AI interaction, AI in research and education, meta-analysis, and careers and well-being at work. [Get in touch](/contact/).
