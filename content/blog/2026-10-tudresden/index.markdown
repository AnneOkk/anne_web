I am very happy to share that since **October 2026**, I have been **Interim Professor (W2) of Organizational Psychology** (*Vertretungsprofessur*) at **TU Dresden** for the winter term 2026/27. It is a special return: I completed my Bachelor's degree in psychology at TU Dresden in 2016.

#### Teaching this term

- **Work and Organizational Psychology in Socio-Technical Systems** – lecture and seminar for Master's students (in English), on how people, technology, and organizations interact at work.
- **Work and Organizational Psychology** – seminar for Bachelor's students (two groups, in German).
- In addition, I continue to teach **Good Scientific Practice** for doctoral researchers and students at TH Augsburg.

More on my courses and teaching materials is on the [teaching page](/teaching/).

#### Research

In Dresden, I continue my research on [human–AI interaction](/research/) – how people evaluate, trust, and work with AI in consequential decisions – and on [thriving, careers, and well-being at work](/research/). I look forward to connecting with colleagues at TU Dresden, including the Else Kröner Fresenius Center for Digital Health, with whom I already collaborate.

Students interested in a thesis on human–AI interaction, AI in research and education, or careers in the age of AI are welcome to [get in touch](/contact/).

#### Looking back

Before Dresden, I spent four great years at **LMU Munich**: first as a postdoc and then as junior research group leader and Principal Investigator of the Human–AI Interaction Group in the Volkswagen Foundation project *AI and the Society of the Future*, followed by a Bavarian Equal Opportunities Fellowship. Many thanks to Eva Lermer, Susanne Gaube, and the whole team.

I was also selected for the Zia-ZEIT Fellowship *Visible Women in Science* in 2026, which I declined because of the interim professorship.
