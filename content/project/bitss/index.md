The [Berkeley Initiative for Transparency in the Social Sciences (BITSS)](https://www.bitss.org/) promotes transparency, reproducibility, and openness in social science research. As a **BITSS Catalyst** (since 2023), I develop and disseminate training materials on open and reproducible research practices. A **BITSS Catalyst Grant** (2024) supported the development of open science teaching material.

My workshops on reproducible data analysis with R and Quarto, and on good scientific practice, are available on the [talks and workshops page](/talk/).
