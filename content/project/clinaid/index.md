The project *AI and the Society of the Future*, funded by the **Volkswagen Foundation** (total project volume €1.5M), aimed to improve clinical decision-making and reduce medical errors by increasing the usability of AI-enabled health technology. Providing healthcare professionals with better technology should not only reduce error rates but also free up time for direct patient interaction.

From 2023 to 2025, I was **Principal Investigator of the Munich work package**, taking over from Prof. Dr. Susanne Gaube on her appointment to University College London, and co-led the **Human–AI Interaction Group at LMU Munich** with Prof. Dr. Eva Lermer. The team included one postdoc, two PhD students, and several research assistants, and we collaborated with partners at MIT and the University of Toronto.

#### Selected outputs

- A meta-analysis of the predictors of clinicians' intention to use AI-enabled clinical decision support systems ([*JMIR*, 2024]({{< relref "/blog/2024-01-01-metautaut" >}}))
- Reviews of AI-enabled precision psychiatry patents ([2023]({{< relref "/blog/2023-02-01-patentreview" >}})) and regulated AI decision support products for mental healthcare ([*Artificial Intelligence in Medicine*, 2025]({{< relref "/blog/2025-01-productreview" >}}))
- Mixed-methods, interview, and experimental studies with mental health practitioners, ophthalmologists, psychotherapy trainees, and patients – see the [publications page](/publications/)
