Together with Prof. Dr. Sarah Diefenbach (LMU Munich), I received funding from the **Virtuelle Hochschule Bayern (vhb)** to develop SMART teaching units on Open Science (€18,000). The units – *Mastering Scientific Reporting and Collaborative Data Management* – are short, interactive H5P learning activities that students and researchers can work through on their own or that lecturers can integrate into their courses.

Topics include transparent reporting of research, organizing and documenting research data, and working on data and code collaboratively in a reproducible way. The units are openly available via the [vhb SMART platform](https://smart.vhb.org/edu-sharing/components/collections?scope=EDU_ALL&id=45ac7639-5d64-42bb-913c-637734423b45).
