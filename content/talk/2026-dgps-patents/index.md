**Authors:** Anne-Kathrin Kleine, Julia Cecil, Insa Schaffernak, Eva Lermer, and Eesha Kokje

AI-enabled clinical decision support systems promise more precise diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment selection in mental healthcare. Yet there is a long way from an invention to a tool that clinicians actually use. In this talk, we triangulated three perspectives on that pathway:

- **Innovation** – what is being developed, as documented in patents on AI-enabled precision psychiatry tools ([patent review]({{< relref "/blog/2023-02-01-patentreview" >}}));
- **Translation** – which tools have made it to the market as regulated products and how well their evidence base supports their use ([product review]({{< relref "/blog/2025-01-productreview" >}}));
- **Adoption** – how practitioners perceive these tools and what facilitates or hinders their use ([practitioner studies]({{< relref "/blog/2025-04-practitioners" >}})).
