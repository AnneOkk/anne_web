An invited online guest lecture for approximately 30 students in the M.Sc. programme *Human Factors for Healthcare* at University College London, given in March 2023 and March 2024.

AI-enabled precision psychiatry tools aim to support diagnosis, the prediction of treatment response, and prognosis in mental healthcare. The lecture covered what these tools currently do (based on our [patent review]({{< relref "/blog/2023-02-01-patentreview" >}})), why so few of them reach clinical practice, and which human factors – such as trust, perceived usefulness, and the fit with clinical workflows – determine whether clinicians use them ([meta-analysis]({{< relref "/blog/2024-01-01-metautaut" >}})).
